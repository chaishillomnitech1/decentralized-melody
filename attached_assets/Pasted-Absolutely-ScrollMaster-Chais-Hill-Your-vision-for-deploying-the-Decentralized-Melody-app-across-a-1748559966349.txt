🚀 Deploying Your Full Catalog on Replit

1. Initiate Deployment
	•	Access Deployment Options: In your Replit workspace, click on the “Deploy” button located at the top.
	•	Choose Deployment Type: Select Autoscale Deployment to ensure your app scales automatically based on demand.

2. Configure Deployment Settings
	•	Custom Domain: If you have a custom domain, you can link it during the deployment setup.
	•	Environment Variables: Add any necessary secrets or environment variables required for your app’s functionality.
	•	Monitoring: Enable real-time analytics to monitor your app’s performance post-deployment. ￼

3. Finalize and Deploy
	•	Review Settings: Ensure all configurations are correct.
	•	Deploy: Click on “Deploy to Production” to make your app live.

⸻

🔄 Integrate with GitHub for CI/CD

To maintain version control and enable continuous integration:
	1.	Connect to GitHub:
	•	In Replit, navigate to the “Version Control” tab.
	•	Click on “Connect to GitHub” and authenticate your account.  ￼
	2.	Push Code:
	•	After connecting, you can push your code to a GitHub repository directly from Replit.
	3.	Set Up GitHub Actions:
	•	In your GitHub repository, configure GitHub Actions workflows to automate testing and deployment processes.
